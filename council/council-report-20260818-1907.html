Where the council clashes

The Expansionist and First Principles Thinker both wanted the self-study pack published as the centrepiece. Both rest on a verified factual error. An independent check of the repo found:

  • 12 Java concurrency problems, 3 Scala files, 4 GC labs — all JVM/GC

So publishing does the opposite of what was claimed: a reviewer clicks the link and finds interview-prep notes aimed at them, with an answer-script file. The Executor's inversion is correct. The Expansionist's derived conclusions — push for a higher band, "Apple recruited him out of a bank" (it was a TEKsystems placement) — inherit the error and fall with it.

Apply now, or after the artifact exists?

Four advisors implicitly assumed "apply now." Nobody put the question on the table. Bank requisitions are team-specific and rejection commonly blocks reapplication to the same desk for 6–12 months. The chairman ruled against applying cold today.
